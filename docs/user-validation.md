# Five short user conversations

Status: prepared; no interviews have been conducted or invented.

Recruit three people who have bought or followed trading signals and two people who publish them. Obtain permission to take anonymous notes. A participant is not evidence of demand until they describe their own behavior and attempt the product. No outreach has been sent.

## Ten-minute session

Ask about the most recent signal they used, what they paid, what happened after a loss, how they chose the caller and how they would prove the result. Ask for an actual example rather than “would you use this?”. Then give them the preview: choose a caller, explain its record, prepare a follow, describe the fee, cancel, and inspect a settlement receipt. Do not explain the controls before observing their attempt.

Afterward: What would stop you using this? What did you think “net gains” included? What happens to an open loser? What would you do when the keeper is down? Which existing tool would this replace? For creators: would they accept loss carry-forward and a disclosed performance fee, and why?
