# Demo and rehearsal

`round-trip-local.webm` is a real browser recording of the local transaction QA flow. It is a fallback execution recording, without narration. It uses public Anvil test accounts, a demo venue and deliberately controlled prices. Do not label it hosted market performance or a physical mobile test.

## Three-minute narration and shot list

| Time | Shot | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Original cabin landing, public call record | “Signal groups sell conviction. Upside makes the call and its outcome visible. You decide whose call to follow, through your own vault.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Winning, losing and unresolved calls | “Each call is committed before evaluation. Losses stay in the record. A signal score is separate from the money a follower actually earns.” |
| 0:45–1:15 | Review, cancel once, then confirm a follow | “Here is the amount, minimum fill, exposure and fee before signing. Canceling sends nothing. This preview uses test tokens and a demo venue.” |
| 1:15–1:45 | Position, close, expanded receipt | “This local example buys with 50 and closes for 60. A 20% fee on the new gain is 2: 1.8 to the caller and 0.2 to the protocol. Every amount comes from the settlement transaction.” |
| 1:45–2:10 | Loss and partial recovery | “Closed losses carry forward. A recovery below the previous peak earns no new fee. Open losses and gas are excluded, and fees already paid are not refunded.” |
| 2:10–2:35 | Actual plugin refusal then allowed action, if host ready | “The agent applies caller and spending rules; the vault has its own exposure cap. Identity describes the agent. It doesn't magically enforce its policy.” If actual MetaMask approval is unavailable, show the labelled local core test and explicitly say host verification is pending. |
| 2:35–3:00 | Hosted explorer receipts, withdraw, product close | “This public testnet flow followed, closed and withdrew. Mobile hardware and paid sponsor checks are our remaining verification. Upside's promise is a visible record, your own decision and a receipt you can inspect.” |
