# Copy review

The editing pass used the Humanizer checklist: remove vague emphasis, explain actual behavior, and keep the language natural without changing the fee rules.

The first draft kept phrases such as “Good judgment deserves good company,” “Bring your judgment. Keep your agency,” and “Make room for your upside.” Read aloud, they added slogans without helping someone decide what to do. Repeated arrows and signup prompts also made navigation feel busier than the product needed.

The final hero says: “Follow a trader through your own vault. They earn a share of your gains after earlier losses are recovered.” The fee section explains why a winning trade may earn no new fee. The vault section explains signing, limits, closing and withdrawals. Questions cover the remaining decisions before a first follow.

The user's “Conviction is personal” heading remains. Technical constraints such as informational stops, gas, fee accounting, test tokens and smart contract risk stay visible where they affect a decision. Illustrative amounts are labelled. No made-up endorsements, user counts or success statistics were added.

Decorative directional arrows were removed from web and Expo copy. Required actions such as following, closing and withdrawal remain; repeated promotional actions and duplicate guide-page browse prompts were removed.
