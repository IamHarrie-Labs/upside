Creative brief: an original wide editorial illustration of a single aircraft cabin, five rounded windows and five fictional adult traders, each with a laptop, notebook or phone. Fine ink lines, warm ivory paper, forest ink, muted copper and sage; clouds around the lower fuselage. Different expressions and activities, calm rather than promotional. No text, logos, arrows, outside charts, glow or gradients. No reference images were supplied to generation.

Full generation prompt: Create an original production website illustration for Upside, a social trading product. Wide horizontal editorial banner about 3:1 ratio. View of the side of a single aircraft cabin: five softly rounded airplane windows in a row, each showing a different original adult trader inside working calmly at a laptop, notebook, or phone. Hand-drawn sophisticated ink linework with flat warm ivory #f4eee5 paper, forest ink #242820, muted copper #a44927 accents, muted sage. Restrained vintage travel editorial style, architectural precision and witty humane expressions, fine subtle paper grain. One trader wears glasses, one woman has natural curls, another holds a coffee. Clouds partially overlap the lower fuselage and at the margins. Distinct original composition and fictional people, not based on any existing video or artwork. Fuselage fills the horizontal frame, windows are clearly readable at small size, no aircraft nose needed. No typography, text, logos, arrows, charts floating outside windows, glow or gradients. The ivory outer background must integrate with #f4eee5 page. Professional, sharp and usable as a full-width website section. Keep all five windows and characters complete, with margin at both sides.

Web uses a gentle CSS flight animation with pause and reduced-motion controls. Native onboarding uses the still. The Cabin brand mark is a separate repository-native vector.
